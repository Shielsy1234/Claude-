                Help

                One-click start on your PC

                The Daydream launcher starts Ollama and ComfyUI with the right settings, waits until they're ready, and opens Daydream in its own window. When you're done, press S in its window to shut both down and free your graphics card.

                1. Download the launcher (Windows). If Edge says it isn't commonly downloaded, open the downloads list, then … → Keep.
                2. Double-click Start-Daydream. The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": press More info → Run anyway.
                3. It adds a Daydream shortcut to your desktop and Start menu. Use that from then on; you can delete the downloaded file.

                It expects ComfyUI Desktop on port 8000 (it also tries 8188) and Ollama in its usual place. It's a plain text file: right-click → Edit to see exactly what it does or change the settings at the top.

                Set up ComfyUI

                Videos (and the fastest images) are made by ComfyUI running on your PC. This app sends it your prompt and shows the result.

                1. Install ComfyUI Desktop. When it asks about your graphics card, pick AMD (ROCm) (or NVIDIA if you have one).
                2. Let this app talk to ComfyUI. In Comfy Desktop's instance settings → Startup Args, add --enable-cors-header. For AMD cards also add --use-pytorch-cross-attention (faster). Then restart ComfyUI. Manual installs: start ComfyUI with the same flags.
                3. Download the model files for the video model you want (see ).
                4. In the Video tab → Video engine, enter ComfyUI's address (Desktop: usually http://127.0.0.1:8188 or :8000) and press Check. If the browser asks about devices on your local network, choose Allow.

                Your models folder: in Comfy Desktop's instance settings → Storage (for you: D:\Comfy-Desktop\ComfyUI-Shared\models).

                Video models

                Wan 2.2 5B: quicker to set up (about 18 GB), 24 frames per second. Wan 2.2 14B: the best quality that fits a 16 GB card, better motion and prompt following, 16 frames per second, already in a fast 4-step mode. It needs about 62 GB of files, and 32 GB of RAM is recommended.

                  Keep file names exactly as they are. If a file shows as missing or broken, check it finished downloading (no .part or .crdownload left) and that it wasn't renamed with a (1).

                  Free online (no PC needed)

                  The Phone switch at the top shows only these. They run on free shared online services, so they work anywhere: Free online HD images (Z-Image Turbo, very realistic), the two Free online video models, and Free online chat.

                  • LTX Video: text-to-video or image-to-video. Fast; short clips (2 to 5 seconds).
                  • Wan 2.2 14B Fast: higher quality. It animates a picture: your start image, or one the app makes first from your description (using your Image engine).
                  • Free daily allowance (images and video share it; images use very little): without an account you get very little GPU time. With a free Hugging Face account and token you get noticeably more; it refills over the day. When it runs out you'll see a message; try again later.
                  • Get a token: sign up at huggingface.co/join, then go to Settings → Access Tokens, create a token with Read access, and paste it under Video engine. It's stored only in this browser.
                  • These are public demos run by their creators, so they can be busy, change or go offline. For unlimited, private use, switch to PC and use your own GPU.

                  Faster videos

                  • Draft quality is about twice as fast as Quick: use it to try prompts, then make keepers in Quick or Full 720p.
                  • Speed (5B): Faster and Fastest use fewer steps and reuse work between steps, about 2 to 3 times faster with a small loss of detail.
                  • The 14B already runs in its 4-step mode; for more speed use Draft or Quick and shorter clips.
                  • Keep ComfyUI open between videos: the first one after starting loads the models and is slower.
                  • Close games and heavy apps; if Task Manager shows the GPU's memory full and "shared" memory climbing, it's spilling into RAM and slowing down.

                  Long videos

                  Video models make about 5 seconds at a time, so long videos are built from many 5-second clips joined into one file.

                  • Storyboard: the AI plans a shot list from your idea. With Make a picture for each shot first, each shot is drawn first and then animated, for sharper, more consistent results.
                  • Extend: one continuous shot; each clip continues from the last frame of the one before. Details slowly drift over long lengths.
                  • Progress is saved after every clip. Press Stop any time, even close the app, and press Resume later in the Video tab. Long videos suit an overnight run.

                  Editing pictures

                  Press Edit on any picture (under a result, or in the viewer when you open a saved one), or choose a photo under Edit a picture in the Image tab. Each edit is saved as a new picture, so the original is kept, and you can keep editing the result.

                  • Free online (every engine except ComfyUI, and on your phone): uses Qwen Image Edit on Hugging Face's free GPUs. Describe just the change: "make it night", "add a red scarf", "remove the people in the background", "sharper, more detailed skin". Uses the same free daily allowance as Free online HD.
                  • ComfyUI on your PC (when ComfyUI is your Image engine): redraws the picture with Z-Image, unlimited and private. Describe the whole picture as you want it, and pick how much to change: Small tweak keeps it very close, Big change allows more freedom.

                  ComfyUI images

                  • z_image_turbo_bf16.safetensors → models/diffusion_models
                  • qwen_3_4b.safetensors → models/text_encoders

                  This PC images

                  Runs Z-Image Turbo inside the app on your graphics card: private and unlimited. Needs Chrome or Edge (not Firefox) on a PC with a modern graphics card. The model (5.6 GB) downloads once and is stored by your browser. The first image after opening the app is slower while the model loads onto your GPU.

                  Ollama chat

                  Use any chat model you like, including bigger or less restricted ones, through the free Ollama app.

                  1. Install Ollama (it supports AMD Radeon cards).
                  2. In Command Prompt, download a model, for example ollama pull qwen3:14b. Browse models at ollama.com/search.
                  3. Let this app connect: run setx OLLAMA_ORIGINS "*", then quit Ollama from the system tray and start it again.
                  4. In Chat → AI model → Ollama, press Check and pick your model.

                  Use on your phone

                  Make videos and ComfyUI images on your phone, powered by your PC, at home or away. Uses Tailscale, a free app that links your devices privately and gives your PC a secure address.

                  1. Install Tailscale on your PC and on your iPhone, signed in to the same account.
                  2. In the Tailscale admin page (DNS), turn on MagicDNS and HTTPS Certificates.
                  3. On your PC, in Command Prompt, run tailscale serve --bg 8188 (your ComfyUI port). It shows an address like https://your-pc.tail1234.ts.net.
                  4. On your phone, open this app in Safari (Share → Add to Home Screen), go to Video → Video engine, enter that https:// address and press Check.

                  Your PC must be on with ComfyUI running. Things you make on the phone are saved in the phone's gallery.

                  Memory & characters

                  Memory: when you mention something lasting (your name, likes, projects), the AI saves it and uses it in future chats with every character. Manage it under Memory in the Chat sidebar.

                  Characters: AI personalities with their own avatar, personality, greeting and voice. Create one with + New; they can send pictures of themselves if they have an appearance.

                  Free graphics memory for videos: lets the chat AI and ComfyUI take turns on the graphics card. The chat and image models are unloaded while ComfyUI makes a video, so it has the whole GPU (a message sent meanwhile waits until the video's done), and an idle ComfyUI is asked to unload its models when you chat with Ollama, so replies run at full speed.
