# Daydream Studio

Type what you're picturing and get a realistic image, or a page of ideas.

Everything is in one file, `index.html`. Open it in any browser, or host it for free with GitHub Pages.

## Image engines

| Engine | Cost | Quality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free, no sign-up | Free | Decent realism, up to 768 px, small watermark, one image every 45 seconds |
| Pollinations account | Free daily allowance | Much more lifelike (Z-Image Turbo, FLUX), full resolution, no watermark. Get a key at [enter.pollinations.ai](https://enter.pollinations.ai) |
| OpenAI | Paid per image | GPT Image models. Key from [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys) |

Pick an engine under **Image engine** at the bottom of the form. Keys are saved only in your browser and sent only to the provider you choose.

## Features

- **Look presets** (natural photo, portrait, cinematic, landscape, product shot, night) and an **Extra realism** switch that add camera and lighting details to your prompt.
- **Improve prompt** turns a short idea into a detailed photographic prompt.
- **Ideas mode** brainstorms 6 ideas; **Picture this** turns any idea into an image.
- Every image is saved to the **Saved images** gallery in your browser, with full-size view and download.
